2025INSA LyonTeam project

📡Network Energy Analysis of a Smartphone

How much energy does a phone really spend on TikTok, streaming or a call, and how much of it goes to the cellular radio? Answered with hardware measurements on instrumented phones, then turned into a web simulator.

Read more

Phones had their internal power lines tapped and had logged the battery, the baseband and the RF power amplifier separately, at 64–256 samples/s. Scenarios: short videos, streaming and voice/video calls on 3G, LTE and 5G, both static and on a moving tram across Lyon.

My part: processing multi-million-row recordings in Python (chunked pandas, interactive Plotly charts) and the comparative analysis of app × network × mobility. Scrolling short videos costs about 0.04–0.06 Wh per minute (~3 Wh per hour); no network was consistently cheaper and mobility had no clear effect, with only 1–3 sessions per setup, so these are trends rather than proof.

The results feed a web simulator (Node.js/Express) that estimates the energy, battery percentage and CO₂ of a day of phone use. I added the device battery specs and front-end fixes.

  • Python
  • pandas
  • Plotly
  • Energy measurement
  • 3G / LTE / 5G
  • Node.js
